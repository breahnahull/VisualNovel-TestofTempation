As you leave the path and approach the maiden, a beautiful melody drifts through the air. Seated upon a large stone overlooking the river, she seems almost unreal, as though she belongs to the landscape itself. Her song is gentle and captivating, each note drawing you closer. The worries of your journey fade from your mind, replaced by a growing desire to stand beside her and hear the music more clearly. Only when you are nearly within reach does the truth reveal itself. The maiden is no ordinary traveler, but a siren, and her enchanting song was never meant as a welcome. Entranced by the melody, you fail to notice the danger until it is far too late. The siren rises from the rock, her beautiful guise giving way to something far more sinister. Unable to break free from her spell, you become yet another victim of the creature's haunting song. As silence returns to the riverbank, the siren resumes her seat upon the stone, waiting patiently for the next traveler to wander down the path.
